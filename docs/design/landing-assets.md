# Landing page imagery

Created with the built-in image-generation tool in generation mode for TournyHub.
These are illustrative campaign images, not photographs of customers or events.
The generated PNGs were compressed to WebP at quality 85 for the website.

## Stadium hero

Output: `public/landing/stadium-hero.webp`.

Prompt direction: photoreal cinematic sports campaign; an adult South Asian
football player seen from behind on the right third, in a plain cobalt jersey,
holding a football. Floodlit pitch at blue hour, blue-black navy shadows,
35mm photographic realism. Dark empty left side for headlines. No text or logos.

## Team huddle

Output: `public/landing/team-huddle.webp`.

Prompt direction: landscape editorial sports photography; six adult South Asian
amateur cricket teammates huddling, viewed from behind, arms over shoulders.
Plain royal-blue jerseys with no logos or numbers, local ground at dusk with
floodlights, realistic deep blue-black, silver, and cobalt palette. No text or UI.

## Application icons

`src/app/icon.svg` is the editable vector source, adapted from the existing
TournyHub T shield. The ICO contains 16, 32, 48, 64, and 256 pixel variants;
the Apple touch icon is 180 pixels. No image generation was used for the icons.
